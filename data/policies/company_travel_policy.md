# Company Travel Policy

## 1. Purpose
This policy defines the rules and guidelines for all business-related travel to ensure cost efficiency, employee safety, and operational effectiveness.

## 2. Scope
This policy applies to all employees, contractors, and consultants who travel on behalf of the company for meetings, conferences, training, or client engagements.

## 3. Booking and Approvals
- All business travel must be pre-approved by the employee’s manager.
- Domestic travel does not require executive approval if total cost is under $1,500.
- International travel requires approval from both the employee’s manager and the finance department.
- Travel bookings should be made at least 7 days in advance whenever possible.

## 4. Flights
- Economy class should be booked for flights under 6 hours.
- Business class may be booked for flights longer than 6 hours with manager approval.
- Employees should choose cost-effective flights that do not significantly disrupt work schedules.
- Red-eye flights should be avoided unless they result in significant cost savings.

## 5. Hotels
- Employees should stay in mid-range business hotels whenever available.
- Luxury hotels should be avoided unless required due to availability or safety concerns.
- Hotel stays should be as close as reasonably possible to the work location to minimize commute time.

## 6. Ground Transportation
- Public transportation or ride-sharing services should be used when practical.
- Rental cars may be used when public transport is not feasible.
- Premium car services should be avoided unless required for safety or time-critical travel.

## 7. Travel Time and Scheduling
- Meetings should not be scheduled immediately after long-distance travel.
- A buffer period of at least 1 hour should be allowed after travel before scheduling meetings.
- Employees should not be scheduled for meetings during typical meal hours unless necessary.
- Late-night or early-morning meetings following travel should be avoided.

## 8. Changes and Cancellations
- Travel changes should be minimized to avoid unnecessary costs.
- If travel plans change, affected meetings should be reviewed and rescheduled if needed.
- Last-minute changes (within 24 hours) should be communicated promptly to all stakeholders.

## 9. Health and Well-being
- Employees are encouraged to avoid excessive travel that could lead to fatigue.
- Consecutive days of long travel should be minimized.
- Employees may request schedule adjustments due to travel fatigue.

## 10. Exceptions
- Exceptions to this policy may be granted with appropriate approval.
- All exceptions should be documented and justified.

## 11. Compliance
Failure to comply with this policy may result in denied reimbursement or other corrective action.
