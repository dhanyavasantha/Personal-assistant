# Past Scheduling and Travel Patterns

## Meeting Timing Patterns
- Meetings scheduled after 4:00 PM are frequently rescheduled.
- Morning meetings before 9:30 AM are rarely accepted.
- Meetings between 10:00 AM and 3:00 PM are most successful.

## Lunch-Time Behavior
- Lunch meetings are typically declined unless marked urgent.
- When lunch meetings are accepted, they are usually short (30 minutes).

## Travel-Related Patterns
- Meetings immediately following travel are often postponed.
- Travel days usually have lighter schedules.
- Virtual meetings are preferred on travel-heavy days.

## Rescheduling Behavior
- Meetings affected by last-minute travel changes are commonly rescheduled.
- If conflicts arise, non-critical meetings are moved first.
- The assistant often notifies the user before making rescheduling decisions.

## Notification Sensitivity
- The user expects notifications for any unexpected changes.
- Routine auto-bookings during standard hours usually do not require confirmation.
