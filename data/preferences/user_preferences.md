# Personal Scheduling Preferences

## General Availability
- I prefer meetings to be scheduled between 10:00 AM and 5:00 PM.
- Early morning meetings before 9:30 AM should be avoided unless necessary.
- Meetings after 6:00 PM should be avoided.

## Lunch and Breaks
- I prefer to keep lunch time free whenever possible.
- Lunch meetings are acceptable only for urgent matters.
- A minimum 30-minute break between long meetings is preferred.

## Meeting Style
- Shorter meetings are preferred over long meetings.
- Back-to-back meetings should be minimized.
- Meetings longer than 1 hour should include buffer time afterward.

## Travel Considerations
- I prefer not to have meetings immediately after travel.
- After long travel, lighter schedules are preferred.
- If travel is involved, virtual meetings are preferred when possible.

## Notifications
- Notify me immediately for last-minute changes or cancellations.
- Notify me if a meeting is auto-booked outside my preferred hours.
- Routine bookings during normal hours do not require notification.

## Flexibility
- I am flexible with rescheduling if it improves overall schedule quality.
- For conflicts involving travel or health, always prioritize well-being.
